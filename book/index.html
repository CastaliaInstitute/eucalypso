THE PROPOSITION

Apocalypse is destruction as disclosure.

Eucalypso is not a cheerful opposite to catastrophe. It is the companion ledger: a study of the capacities that buffer, interpret, repair, and transform a civilization under pressure.

Collapse does not create the good. It reveals some of what was hidden, and destroys much else. The work is to recognize and protect the good early enough.
