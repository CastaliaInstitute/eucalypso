01THE THESIS

Apocalypse is a passage, not a personality.

We have been trained to imagine the end as spectacle: the mushroom cloud, the empty highway, the lone survivor. But most endings are slower, stranger, and shared. They are also disclosures.

Eucalypso does not subtract danger from the ledger. It asks a harder question: what is already alive enough to receive the shock—and what must we learn to build?
