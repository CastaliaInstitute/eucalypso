02 / THE CAPACITIES

Four ways to receive a rupture.

Capacity is not one virtue. A place can have reserves but no response, response but no recovery, or recovery without the courage to transform.

04

Transformation

The ability to build better rather than merely restore the failed.

04 / METHOD

A dual ledger, not a net score.

Apocalypso measures hazard, exposure, and vulnerability. Eucalypso measures buffer, response, recovery, and transformation.

No Eucalypso composite is published yet. Every future value must retain its definition, geography, time window, source, confidence, missingness, and transformation rule. Pressure and capacity are not interchangeable and must not be visually collapsed into a single verdict.

Initial source families: WHO Universal Health Coverage data; UNESCO Institute for Statistics / Global Education Monitoring data; UNDRR Sendai Framework Monitor. AI-assisted capability remains a research question, not a population statistic.
